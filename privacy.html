Privacy policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

This policy explains what personal data Arka Glycan (Vaibhav Singh, [POSTAL ADDRESS]) collects through this website, the software and licence sales, and how we use it.

This website

  • The website sets no cookies and uses no analytics, tracking or advertising.
  • The beta form does not send anything to us or to any form service: it opens your own email app with the details you typed, and nothing is sent until you press Send.
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical data such as IP addresses when pages are requested; see GitHub's privacy statement.

The software

  • Arka Glycan processes your spectra and results only on your own computer. It does not send your data, results or usage statistics to us or anyone else.
  • The licence key is checked on your computer, without contacting us.
  • The only network access is the optional one-time download of the Thermo converter from its publisher's GitHub release, when you ask for it.

When you write to us or buy a licence

  • We collect what you send us: your name, institution, email address and message; and for purchases, billing details and the licence details (tier, type, dates).
  • We use these to answer you, issue and deliver licence keys, provide support and updates, send invoices, and meet legal and tax obligations.
  • If you pay online, the payment is processed by our payment provider under its own privacy policy; we do not receive or store your full card or bank details.
  • We do not sell or rent personal data, and we share it only with service providers needed for the purposes above or when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

As long as needed for your licence and support, and afterwards as long as tax and accounting law requires.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your personal data, or withdraw consent to being contacted, by writing to [CONTACT EMAIL]. We handle requests under applicable data-protection law, including India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.
