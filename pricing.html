Subscription or perpetual?

A yearly subscription includes every update and support while it runs. When it ends, Arka Glycan keeps working for 14 more days, then switches to read-only mode: you can still open, view and export every past analysis, but need to renew to start new ones. Your data are never locked or deleted.

A perpetual licence never expires. It includes one year of updates and support; after that, maintenance is about 20% of the licence price a year. Without maintenance the version you have keeps working indefinitely.
