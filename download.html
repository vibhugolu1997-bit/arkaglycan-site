macOS

  • Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or later)
  • macOS 12 Monterey or later
  • About 130 MB of disk space

Windows

  • Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit
  • No administrator rights needed
  • About 170 MB of disk space

Your data

  • Thermo .raw (converter downloaded once, with Thermo's licence)
  • Bruker timsTOF .d (as a .zip)
  • mzML and mzXML

First start

The beta builds are not yet code-signed. On macOS, open System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway once. On Windows, click More info → Run anyway when SmartScreen appears. The user guide walks through both.
