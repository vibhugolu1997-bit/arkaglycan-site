The problem

Annotation by hand is slow

Matching hundreds of MS/MS spectra to glycan compositions in a vendor viewer takes days per batch, and the result depends on who did it.

Pipelines need a programmer

Open-source tools are powerful but expect a command line, converted files and scripting that most glycobiology labs do not have time for.

Calls without evidence

A composition list alone does not say how sure you can be. Reviewers ask for spectra, settings and a reproducible method.

Data that cannot leave the lab

Patient-derived and unpublished data often may not be uploaded to a web service, and many instrument PCs are offline.

How it works

1

Install

One installer for macOS (Apple Silicon) or Windows 10/11. No Python, no command line.

2

Open your file

Thermo .raw, a zipped Bruker timsTOF .d folder, or mzML/mzXML. Or try the bundled example.

3

Check the settings

Validated defaults for permethylated N-glycans, and plain-language settings for RapiFluor, 2-AB, procainamide and O-glycans, positive or negative mode.

4

Get the report

Glycans with confidence tiers, annotated spectra, CSV and Excel downloads, and a Methods paragraph with the exact settings.

See it in action

A two-minute walkthrough of the bundled example: confidence tiers, the results table, annotated MS/MS spectra, exports and the Methods text.
