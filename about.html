About us

Arka Glycan is software for identifying glycans in released-glycan LC-MS/MS data, built for glycobiology and biomarker labs that want reliable glycan reports without a command line. It is developed by Vaibhav Singh and is based in India.

The software matches every MS/MS precursor to glycan compositions, checks the fragment ions, and reports each glycan with a confidence tier, its supporting spectra and a ready-to-paste Methods paragraph. It reads Thermo, Bruker timsTOF and open mzML data, and runs entirely on the user's own computer, so unpublished and patient-derived data never leave the lab.

We test Arka Glycan against public datasets and publish the agreement with the original authors' results, including where it falls short. See validation.
